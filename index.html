Alex Serrano Terre

I am a last-year Data Science and Mathematics undergraduate at CFIS (Politechnic University of Catalonia). Currently, a Research Scholar at MATS, advised by Scott Emmons from Google DeepMind.

Previously, I've conducted research at the UC Berkeley Center for Human Compatible AI, advised by Scott Emmons. I've been a Research Fellow at AI Safety Camp, working on fundamental interpretability research supervised by Kola Ayonrinde and a Research Fellow at SPAR, working on white-box control measures supervised by Aryan Bhatt.

I am interested in making AI models safer and more robust, and developing effective monitoring measures that can robustly scale to frontier language models. If you are interested in collaborating, please reach out.
